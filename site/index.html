About

I'm a final-year Electronics and Communication student at R. V. College of Engineering in Bengaluru. My focus is digital hardware: writing RTL in SystemVerilog, verifying it with assertions and coverage, and bringing it up on real boards like the Basys-3 FPGA, STM32 and ESP32.

I also build the software around the hardware. Since December 2025 I've been a technical support engineer at Thinkads.co, first as an intern and now as a freelancer, shipping full-stack apps for clients. My projects tend to cross that line: an FPGA parking controller, an ESP32 that keeps a live digital twin of a bus, and a voice assistant that answers in Kannada from a model running locally.
